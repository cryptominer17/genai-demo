## Section 3 — Market Commentary

**Macro Outlook: Navigating a Late-Cycle Environment**

The first quarter of 2026 continued to be characterized by resilient, if decelerating, U.S. economic growth. The Federal Reserve held its policy rate steady at 4.25%–4.50%, signaling a cautious "higher-for-longer" posture as core services inflation remains sticky in the 3.1%–3.4% range. Labor markets have softened modestly, with the unemployment rate edging up to 4.3%, but consumer spending remains supported by above-trend wage gains. For a trust with a 10–15 year horizon, this environment argues for maintaining meaningful equity exposure while selectively extending fixed income duration to lock in real yields before any anticipated rate normalization cycle.

**Fixed Income: An Improving Opportunity Set**

Investment-grade corporate spreads have compressed modestly YTD, but intermediate Treasuries continue to offer historically attractive real yields north of 1.8%. For the Hartwell Trust, the upcoming Q4 2026 beneficiary distribution creates a specific liability to plan around. We recommend laddering a portion of the fixed income sleeve into 18–36 month Treasury notes to match the distribution timeline, reducing reinvestment risk while maintaining yield.

**International Equities: Selective Opportunity**

Developed international markets — particularly European large-cap equities and Japanese exporters — have outperformed U.S. peers year-to-date on a currency-adjusted basis, driven by continued corporate earnings improvement and attractive relative valuations (forward P/E ~13x vs. ~20x for the S&P 500). We recommend a modest increase in international exposure via a low-cost factor ETF vehicle available on the Fidelity Institutional platform, maintaining currency-hedged exposure to reduce volatility.

## Section 4 — Recommended Actions

Based on the Q1 2026 portfolio review and the trust's evolving objectives, Reeves Wealth Partners recommends the following five actions for trustee approval:

1. **Trim U.S. Equities (−4%):** Reduce domestic equity exposure from 42% to 38% by trimming large-cap growth positions with embedded gains. Estimated capital gains impact to be reviewed with the trust's tax counsel prior to execution.

2. **Increase International Equities (+2%):** Reallocate proceeds from the equity trim to a currency-hedged developed market ETF. Target: iShares MSCI EAFE ETF or equivalent available on Fidelity Institutional's model portfolio platform.

3. **Extend Fixed Income Duration (+2%):** Shift 2% of portfolio from money market / short-duration holdings into intermediate U.S. Treasury notes (2–3 year maturity) to capture current yield levels ahead of potential rate cuts in late 2026 or early 2027.

4. **Establish Q4 2026 Distribution Reserve:** Earmark $420,000 (5% of AUM) in a separate short-duration FDIC-insured cash position to fund anticipated beneficiary distributions without requiring forced equity liquidation.

5. **Review Estate & Transfer Tax Exposure:** Given anticipated legislative changes to estate tax exemption thresholds, the trust's legal counsel should be engaged by Q3 2026 to assess whether additional planning vehicles (e.g., GRAT, irrevocable life insurance trust) are appropriate.

> **Action Required:** Trustee signature required on the enclosed investment policy statement addendum before execution of items 1–3. Items 4 and 5 require no immediate authorization but are flagged for trustee awareness.
